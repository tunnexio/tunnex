### Product answer lifecycle versus office DNS ingress

The office client does not need to contact the WireGuard-bound product DNS listener to populate a product FQDN generation. Production `apps/api/cmd/server/main.go` starts `fqdnresolver.Scheduler` with `GatewayDNSRPCTransport` and the durable gateway DNS mailbox. `scheduler.go` periodically selects due work, resolves through `LookupWork`, and publishes or withdraws the generation. The selected gateway processes the authenticated request in `fqdnrpc.Responder.Handle`, preserving resolver context/profile/connection revision/source and deadline; bound IPsec transport validates live authority. This is independent of the office client's DNS query and remains required evidence.
