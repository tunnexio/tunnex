# S20.5 foreign unfinished enrollment — contract-preserving repair

Status: FIX BEFORE WALK, finding 3; implementation authorized within locked
D14/D14o owner-bound identity. No multi-user identity-storage redesign.

D14o explicitly refuses a different user at the same normalized origin. A
foreign unfinished anchor must not project its organization, owner, keys,
device identifier, or preferences into the new user's session, and logout
must not overwrite or destroy unresolved identity. Origin-keyed final state
already enforces the same bound-owner restriction.

The defect is that this mandatory refusal is discovered only after helper and
lifecycle side effects, is hidden by the renderer, and remove-device falsely
reports absence. The narrow repair is:

- prove foreign-anchor ownership in the read-only connect preflight, before
  helper installation, epoch changes, quiescing, or monitor changes; retain
  the later check as a drift guard;
- expose only a boolean/static blocked reason to the current renderer, never
  foreign identity data or an anchored organization;
- render an actionable static explanation to sign out and return with the
  originating account to resume recovery; do not promise immediate abandon
  across a new credential unless its rebind proof actually permits it;
- refuse remove/abandon early and honestly for a foreign anchor, preserving
  its exact bytes and making no control-plane/helper mutation; and
- prove that the originating account can still recover and that normal
  no-anchor/same-owner flows remain valid.

This fixes the invisible lockout/false-success path, not independent parallel
enrollment for two users at one origin. Independent users would require a
feature-sized owner-keyed managed-identity model, final-store migration, and
separate policy decisions; merely re-keying unfinished anchors moves the
collision to the final store. That expansion is not silently included here.
The resulting UI boundary and regressions must earn independent review.
