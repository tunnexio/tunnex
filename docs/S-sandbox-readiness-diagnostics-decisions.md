# Sandbox readiness diagnostics

The native trial established repeated initial rejection at the policy acknowledgement gate, while Resume logged only its outer stage. This source-only refinement preserves the specific nested launch stage and gives the existing operator reconciliation log a bounded typed policy-gate diagnostic. It changes neither readiness admission nor timings, policy reporting, live configuration, credentials, runtime activation or lease lifetime.

Policy failures retain their existing `errors.Is` identity and nil acknowledgement result. Classify the existing predicates in their current order. Log only a fixed reason, trusted binding-mode enum, eligible public selected-node ID, desired-hash-known and hash-equality booleans, known report age, normalized health-kind enum, and gate/policy-health evaluation durations. Unknown compilation/report states remain unknown. Policy-health timing includes its full reader evaluation; it is not advertised as a pure compiler timer. No policy/hash content, raw cause/error, capability payload, private key or token enters the log. Invalid/cross-org identities are omitted.

Acceptance requires regression evidence that hash mismatch, missing/stale/future reports, apply/refusal failure, missing health and invalid membership still deny Ready; permitted local advisory site health remains permitted; resumed failures expose their original inner stage; and malicious hash/capability/health-kind/cause sentinels cannot enter serialized operator logs. Existing race/admission tests and focused community/enterprise checks apply. A later approved trial must capture typed per-gate reason/node/timing together with unchanged canonical Ready, resource, offline-expiry and cleanup proofs. No trial or deployment follows from these source changes.
